# Filled Peppers

## Ingredients

- 1_ onion
- 1_ clove garlic
- 500g minced meat
- 100g rice
- 1_ egg
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 Tbsp pepper
- 1 Tbsp paprika
- 10_ peppers
- 1 Tbsp butter
- 1 Tbsp flour
- 500ml vegetable broth
- 2 Tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tsp sugar

## Steps

1. Mince onion and garlic
2. Mix onion, garlic, minced meat, rice and egg, add salt, pepper and paprika
3. Cut off the tops of the peppers and remove seeds and walls
4. Fill peppers with mixed filling, make meat balls out of any remaining filling
5. Melt butter in a pot, stir in flour
6. Add broth and tomato paste, bring to a boil, reduce heat and simmer for 3 min
7. Add peppers into the sauce and simmer for about 40 min
