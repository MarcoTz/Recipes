# Paprika Tofuballs

## Ingredients

### Tofu balls

- 400g tofu
- 1_ onion
- 1_ clove garlic
- 2 Tbsp soy sauce
- 2 Tbsp rolled oats
- 1 Tbsp starch
- 1 tsp sweet paprika
- 1 tsp mustard
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 Tbsp pepper

### Sauce

- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- 1_ red onion
- 2_ bell peppers
- 2 Tbsp tomato paste
- 100ml vegetable broth
- 200ml cream
- 1 tsp sweet paprika
- 0.5 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 Tbsp pepper
- 1 tsp chili flakes (optional)

## Steps

1. Drain tofu, mince onion and garlic and peppers
2. Mix ingredients for the balls, and let rest for about 10min
3. Form into balls and pan-fry the balls
4. For the sauce, sautee onions and peppers, add tomato paste
5. add broth and cream, then spices
6. let simmer for about 10min
