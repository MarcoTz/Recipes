# Waffles

## Ingredients

- 3_ eggs
- 125g sugar
- 15g vanilla sugar
- 125g butter
- 1 Tbsp baking powder
- 250g flour
- 250ml milk
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp neutral oil

## Steps

1. Mix sugar, vanilla sugar and eggs until foamy
2. Mix in butter
3. Add milk, flour, baking powder and salt, mix into a thick batter
4. Bake in waffle iron
