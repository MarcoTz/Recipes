# Marmelade

## Ingredients

- 500g fruit/berries
- 250g gelling sugar
- 1 Tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp salt

## Steps

1. Clean fruit and cut into small pieces
2. Mix fruit and sugar in a pot, let rest for 2 hours
3. Add lemon juice and cook fruit mixture for about 4 min
4. Fill marmelade into clean glasses
