# Nugget Gnocchi Casserole

## Ingredientsq

- 10-12_ Chicken Nuggets
- 500g Gnocchi
- 600g Spinach
- 500ml Cream (1)
- 250g grated cheese
- 1tsp salt
- 2tsp pepper

## Steps

1. Combine Spinach and Gnocchi in casserole form
2. Add spices to cream, pour over gnocchi
3. Cover with cheese and nuggets
4. Bake for 20min ad 200C upper/lower

## Notes

(1) Instead of using cream and spinach, you can also use creamed spinach
directly
