# Currant Cheesecake

## Ingredients

### Streusel

- 330g flour
- 200g butter
- 150g sugar
- 1 tsp salt

### Filling

- 300g currants
- 750g quark
- 90g sugar
- 1 Tbsp vanilla sugar
- 3_ eggs
- 100ml milk
- 75 ml oil

## Steps

1. Mix ingredients for streusel and knead to a dough, cool for 30min
2. Preheat oven to 180C upper/lower
3. Combine filling ingredients until well mixed
4. half streusel dough, spread half on a oiled baking form
5. add filling
6. add remaining streusel on top
7. bake for 55min
