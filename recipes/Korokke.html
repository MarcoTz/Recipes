Korokke

  • 800 ml frying oil

Filling

  • 900 g potatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 1 Tbsp neutral oil
  • 500 g ground beef or mixed vegetables
  • 1 Tbsp salt
  • 0.5 Tbsp pepper
  • 1 Tbsp butter

Breading

  • 60 g flour
  • 120 g panko
  • 3 eggs
  1. Wash, peel and cut quarter potatoes, then cook them until soft
  2. Mince and pan-fry onion, then add meat and spices
  3. Once potatoes are done, remove water and keep cooking on very low heat, until
  5. Mash potatoes then mix with the remaining filling
  6. Form mixture into balls of about 5cm diameter
  7. Let them cool down completely, then rest in refrigerator for 10-15min
  8. Roll each korokke in flour, then egg, then panko
  9. Fry in oil until orange-brown

Notes

Optionally add grated cheese to the mixture
